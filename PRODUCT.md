# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Travian players use TTQ while managing time-sensitive scheduled actions during play. They need controls that stay reliable and quick to recover when page state or saved panel positions become inconvenient.

## Product Purpose

TTQ schedules delayed game actions in one installable userscript. Success means players can understand, control, and recover their queue without losing time or risking unintended actions.

## Brand Personality

Practical, calm, dependable.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, experimental controls, hidden recovery actions, and styling that competes with the game interface.

## Design Principles

- Keep time-sensitive actions reachable.
- Prefer familiar controls over novelty.
- Make recovery from user-interface failures immediate.
- Preserve the game context as the visual priority.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, and keyboard-accessible actions as the default baseline.
